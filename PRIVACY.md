# Privacy Policy — Fet Features

Last updated: February 2026

## Summary

This browser extension does not collect, store, transmit or sell any
personal data. There are no analytics, no tracking, no external servers.

## What the extension does

The extension runs only on fetlife.com. It reads elements on the pages you
visit — notifications, the home feed and the message-requests list —
locally in your browser, in order to classify them (e.g. notification type,
loved-picture grids, the sender's self-identified gender label on requests)
and then hide or resize them according to your settings. All processing
happens inside the browser; no page content ever leaves your device.

## What is stored

The only data the extension stores are your own settings: which
notification types to hide, the home-feed thumbnail mode, whether to hide
requests from a given gender, and the global on/off switch. These are saved
via the browser's built-in extension storage (`storage.sync`), which may
sync them across your own browser profile through your browser vendor's
account sync (Mozilla or Google), subject to that vendor's privacy policy.
The extension itself has no access to any server.

## What is NOT collected

- No browsing history, page content or message content
- No personal or account information
- No usage statistics or telemetry
- No cookies, fingerprinting or identifiers

## Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because no data is collected. The
extension makes no network requests of its own.

## Changes

Changes to this policy will be published in this repository. Since the
extension collects nothing, changes are expected to be editorial only.
