# Privacy Policy — Fet Features

Last updated: November 5, 2026

## Summary

This browser extension does not collect, transmit or sell any data. There
are no analytics, no tracking, no external servers. Everything it stores
stays inside your browser.

## What the extension does

The extension runs only on fetlife.com. It reads elements on the pages you
visit — notifications, the home feed, the message-requests list, comment
widgets and group member pages — locally in your browser, in order to
classify them (e.g. notification type, loved-picture grids, the sender's
self-identified gender label on requests, usernames on member lists) and
then hide or resize them according to your settings. All processing
happens inside the browser; no page content ever leaves your device.

## What is stored

Two kinds of data, both only on your device or in your own browser
profile:

1. **Your settings** — which notification types to hide, thumbnail mode,
   request and badge toggles, and the global on/off switch. Saved via the
   browser's extension storage (`storage.sync`), which may sync them
   across your own browser profile through your browser vendor's account
   sync (Mozilla or Google), subject to that vendor's privacy policy.
2. **Your mute list** — usernames you chose to mute, either one page at a
   time from group member pages you visited yourself, or via file import.
   Each entry records where it came from (e.g. which group's member list).
   The list is saved via local extension storage (`storage.local`) on the
   device only; it is never synced or transmitted anywhere by the
   extension. You can export it to a text file, import such a file, or
   delete the list at any time from the popup. Uninstalling the extension
   deletes it.

The extension itself has no access to any server.

## What is NOT collected

- No browsing history, page content or message content
- No personal or account information
- No usage statistics or telemetry
- No cookies, fingerprinting or identifiers
- Nothing is transmitted: the mute list and all settings stay in your
  browser

## Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because no data leaves your browser.
The extension makes no network requests of its own. If you export your
mute list to a file and share that file, that sharing is your own action
outside the extension.
